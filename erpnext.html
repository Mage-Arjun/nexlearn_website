ERPNext overview

One platform for the whole business.

ERPNext is an open-source ERP that connects sales, accounting, inventory, manufacturing, projects and human resources on a single system, so every team works from the same records. No more switching between disconnected tools or re-entering the same data in multiple places.

See our programmes →

Connected business modules

Everything works together.

ERPNext covers every core business function. Each module shares data with the others, so a sales order flows automatically into delivery, invoicing and accounting.

  • CRM & SalesManage leads, customers, quotations and sales orders from first contact to payment.
  • PurchasingManage suppliers, request quotes, create purchase orders and track goods receipt.
  • AccountingChart of accounts, invoicing, payments, bank reconciliation and financial reporting.
  • Stock & InventoryTrack stock across warehouses, manage batches and serial numbers, and set reorder levels.
  • ManufacturingBill of materials, work orders, production planning and quality inspection.
  • Human ResourcesEmployee records, attendance, leave management and payroll processing.
  • ProjectsProject planning, task tracking, time recording and project profitability analysis.
