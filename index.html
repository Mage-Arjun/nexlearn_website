Understanding ERPNext

One platform for the whole business.

ERPNext is an open-source ERP that connects sales, accounting, inventory, manufacturing, projects and human resources on a single system, so every team works from the same records.

  • CRM & SalesManage leads, customers and sales.
  • PurchasingManage suppliers and procurement.
  • AccountingSimplify finance and compliance.
  • Stock & InventoryTrack and manage inventory in real time.
  • ManufacturingPlan production and track quality.
  • Human ResourcesManage employees and attendance.
  • ProjectsPlan, track and deliver projects.
