01Who we are

Nexlearn offers online ERPNext professional programmes, delivered with Nexora Fintec and FinRock Academy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Our office: Office No. 2107, 1st Floor, Phase 2, HiLITE Business Park, Poovangal, Pantheeramkavu, Kozhikode, Keralam – 673014.

02Information we collect

We collect only what we need to answer your questions and run our programmes.

  • What you give us: your name, WhatsApp or phone number, the course you are interested in and any message you write, when you use the enquiry form. If you call, message us on WhatsApp or email us, we also receive whatever you choose to share in that conversation.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends to any website, such as IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested. Our hosting provider may keep this in standard server logs for security and reliability.

We do not ask for payment card details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information through this website.

03How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and help you choose between the Functional Learning and Development courses.
  • To share batch dates, schedules, fee details and joining instructions.
  • To contact you about your enquiry or enrolment by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To keep the website secure, fix problems and understand which pages are useful.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to send marketing to people who have not asked to hear from us.

04Consent

When you submit the enquiry form or contact us, you agree that we may use your information as described here to respond to you. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us at the address below. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it, and we may need to keep limited records where the law requires.

05Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed to deal with your enquiry or deliver the programme:

  • Delivery partners: Nexora Fintec and FinRock Academy, who help deliver the programmes.
  • Service providers: the services that host this website, process the enquiry form, and carry our calls, WhatsApp messages and email. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities: where we are required to by law or a valid legal request.

06Third-party services on this website

Some parts of this website load content from other companies, which means they may receive your IP address and basic browser details when the page loads:

  • Google Fonts for the typefaces used on the site.
  • Google Maps for the map on our location section, and when you open directions.
  • WhatsApp when you choose to message us there.
  • Our enquiry form processor, which receives what you type into the form when you send it.

These companies have their own privacy policies. We do not currently use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this website. If that changes, we will update this page first.

07Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store small amounts of data needed for the site to work, and the third-party services above may set their own. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

08How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry, run your enrolment if you join, and meet legal or accounting requirements. When we no longer need them, we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your details sooner.

09Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections to this website and limiting who can see enquiries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, where we no longer need it;
  • stop contacting you, or withdraw your consent;
  • deal with a complaint about how we handle your information.

Write to us using the contact details below and tell us what you would like. We may need to confirm who you are first.

11Children

Our programmes are meant for adults and students who are old enough to enrol on their own. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you. If you believe a child has sent us personal information without a parent or guardian’s consent, let us know and we will delete it.

12Where your information is processed

Some of the service providers we use may store or process information on servers outside India. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure it is handled with appropriate care.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will say so on the website.
